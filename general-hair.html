Solutions for Common Hair Concerns

🌊 Dry & Damaged Hair

Causes: Heat styling, chemical treatments, environmental stress

Solutions:

  • Use moisturizing shampoo and conditioner
  • Apply weekly hair masks
  • Limit heat styling
  • Use leave-in treatments

💧 Oily Scalp

Causes: Overactive sebaceous glands, product buildup

Solutions:

  • Clarifying shampoo weekly
  • Lightweight, non-greasy products
  • Dry shampoo between washes
  • Avoid touching hair frequently

❄️ Dandruff & Flakes

Causes: Dry scalp, fungal growth, product sensitivity

Solutions:

  • Anti-dandruff shampoo with zinc or ketoconazole
  • Scalp exfoliation treatment
  • Tea tree oil products
  • Stay hydrated and manage stress

📉 Hair Loss & Thinning

Causes: Genetics, stress, nutritional deficiency, hormones

Solutions:

  • Growth-stimulating serums
  • Biotin and vitamin supplements
  • Gentle scalp massage
  • Consult healthcare professional

Build Your Hair Care Routine

1

Cleanse

Choose a shampoo suited to your hair type. Wash 2-3 times per week or as needed.

2

Condition

Apply conditioner from mid-length to ends. Leave for 2-3 minutes before rinsing.

3

Treat

Use a hair mask or treatment weekly for extra nourishment and repair.

4

Protect

Apply heat protectant before styling and use leave-in products for daily protection.

5

Style

Use appropriate styling products for your desired look and hair type.
