Afro Hair Care Tips

💧 Hydration is Key

Keep hair moisturized with water-based products and seal with oils. The LOC method (Liquid, Oil, Cream) works wonders.

🛡️ Protect at Night

Use a satin or silk bonnet/pillowcase to reduce friction and maintain moisture while sleeping.

✂️ Regular Trims

Trim split ends every 8-12 weeks to maintain healthy hair growth and prevent breakage.

🌊 Deep Condition Weekly

Weekly deep conditioning treatments help maintain elasticity, strength, and moisture balance.
