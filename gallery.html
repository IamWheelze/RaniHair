Instagram Integration Tips

📱 Using Instagram App

To share from app:

  • Open your post on Instagram
  • Tap the paper airplane icon (Share)
  • Select "Copy Link"
  • Paste the link in the "Add Instagram Post" box above

💻 Using Web Browser

To get embed code:

  • Open Instagram post in browser
  • Click the three dots (...)
  • Select "Embed"
  • Copy the embed code
  • Paste in the "Embed Code Generator" section

📤 Posting to Instagram Stories

Share your website:

  • Click "Generate Share Link" button
  • Copy the generated link
  • Open Instagram and create a story
  • Add a link sticker with your URL

🎨 Best Practices

For better integration:

  • Use high-quality images on Instagram
  • Tag posts with #RaniHair
  • Update gallery regularly
  • Engage with followers who share your posts
